# Privacy Policy – Workshop Spam Filter for CS2

This extension does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data.

- It runs only on `https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/browse/*`.
- To find spam, it reads the map titles, descriptions and author IDs that Steam already sends to your browser, and it requests the following result pages from steamcommunity.com (the same requests your browser would make when you page through the workshop).
- Your settings (thresholds, keywords, blocked and allowed authors) are stored with `chrome.storage.sync` in your own browser profile. The list of maps already shown is kept in `sessionStorage` and deleted when you close the tab.
- The extension makes no requests to any server other than steamcommunity.com and sends no analytics or telemetry.
